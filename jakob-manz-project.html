The Jakob Manz Project

With their fresh and compelling sound, The Jakob Manz Project is one of the most successful bands in young German jazz. Their debut album, „Natural Energy,“ was released in April 2020 under the renowned label ACT. The band has won over the hearts of their audience through numerous concerts, including performances at major festivals such as the Elbjazz Festival, Leverkusener Jazztage, LOTOS Jazzfestival (Poland), and Jazz Baltica.
